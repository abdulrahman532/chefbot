# Classic Caesar Salad
## Category: Salad
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 5 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Crisp romaine lettuce tossed with creamy Caesar dressing, garlic croutons, and shaved parmesan.

## Ingredients
- 1 large head of romaine lettuce, washed and chopped
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 cup croutons
- 1/2 cup Caesar dressing (store-bought or homemade)
- 1 garlic clove, halved
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Freshly ground black pepper

## Instructions
1. Rub the inside of a large salad bowl with the cut side of the garlic clove, then discard the garlic.
2. Add the chopped romaine lettuce to the bowl.
3. Drizzle with the Caesar dressing and toss gently to coat the leaves evenly.
4. Add the croutons and parmesan cheese, tossing once more.
5. Grind fresh black pepper over the top and serve immediately.
