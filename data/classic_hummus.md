# Classic Creamy Hummus
## Category: Appetizer
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 0 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Super smooth, creamy homemade hummus made with chickpeas, tahini, garlic, and fresh lemon.

## Ingredients
- 1 can (400g) chickpeas, drained (reserve 2 tbsp of chickpea liquid)
- 1/3 cup tahini (sesame paste)
- 1/4 cup fresh lemon juice
- 1 small garlic clove, minced
- 2 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1/2 tsp ground cumin
- Salt to taste
- Pinch of paprika and extra olive oil for serving

## Instructions
1. In a food processor, combine tahini and lemon juice. Process for 1 minute to whip the tahini.
2. Add olive oil, minced garlic, cumin, and salt. Process for 30 seconds.
3. Add half the chickpeas and process for 1 minute. Scrape down the sides.
4. Add the remaining chickpeas and process for 1-2 minutes until thick and smooth.
5. If the hummus is too thick, add the reserved chickpea liquid 1 teaspoon at a time until desired consistency is reached.
6. Transfer to a bowl, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with a pinch of paprika, and serve with pita bread.
