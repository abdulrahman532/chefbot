# Classic Homemade Apple Pie
## Category: Dessert
## Prep Time: 30 mins
## Cook Time: 50 mins
## Difficulty: Hard

## Description
Flaky pie crust filled with sweet, spiced apples baked to golden perfection.

## Ingredients
- 2 pre-made pie crust rolls
- 6 large apples (such as Granny Smith), peeled, cored, and sliced
- 3/4 cup granulated sugar
- 2 tbsp all-purpose flour
- 1 tsp ground cinnamon
- 1/4 tsp ground nutmeg
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- 1 tbsp butter, cut into small pieces
- 1 egg, beaten (for egg wash)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 220°C (425°F).
2. In a large bowl, toss sliced apples with sugar, flour, cinnamon, nutmeg, and lemon juice.
3. Fit one pie crust into a 9-inch pie plate. Fill with the apple mixture and dot the top with butter pieces.
4. Roll out the second pie crust and place it over the apples. Trim and crimp the edges to seal.
5. Cut a few slits in the top crust to allow steam to escape.
6. Brush the top crust with beaten egg.
7. Bake for 20 minutes, then reduce oven temperature to 190°C (375°F) and bake for another 30-35 minutes until crust is golden and apples are tender.
