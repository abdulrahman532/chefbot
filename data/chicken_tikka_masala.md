# Chicken Tikka Masala
## Category: Indian
## Prep Time: 20 mins
## Cook Time: 30 mins
## Difficulty: Hard

## Description
Tender pieces of marinated chicken tikka cooked in a rich, creamy, spiced tomato sauce.

## Ingredients
- 800g boneless chicken thighs, cut into bite-sized pieces
- 1 cup plain yogurt
- 2 tbsp lemon juice
- 2 tbsp garam masala
- 1 tbsp ground cumin
- 1 tbsp ground turmeric
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1 large onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 tbsp grated fresh ginger
- 1 can (400g) tomato puree
- 1 cup heavy cream
- Fresh cilantro for garnish
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. In a bowl, combine yogurt, lemon juice, 1 tbsp garam masala, cumin, turmeric, and salt. Add chicken and marinate for at least 1 hour (or overnight).
2. Preheat oven to 200°C (400°F). Place marinated chicken on skewers and bake for 15 minutes until slightly charred.
3. Heat oil in a large pot over medium heat. Saute onion, garlic, and ginger until soft and fragrant.
4. Add the remaining 1 tbsp garam masala and tomato puree. Simmer for 10 minutes.
5. Stir in the heavy cream and baked chicken pieces. Simmer for another 10 minutes until sauce thickens.
6. Garnish with fresh cilantro and serve hot with basmati rice or naan bread.
