# Fluffy Buttermilk Pancakes
## Category: Breakfast
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 15 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Light, fluffy, and golden-brown pancakes, perfect for weekend breakfast.

## Ingredients
- 2 cups all-purpose flour
- 2 tbsp sugar
- 2 tsp baking powder
- 1 tsp baking soda
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 2 cups buttermilk
- 2 large eggs
- 50g unsalted butter, melted
- Maple syrup and butter for serving

## Instructions
1. In a large bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, baking soda, and salt.
2. In another bowl, whisk buttermilk, eggs, and melted butter.
3. Pour the wet ingredients into the dry ingredients and stir gently with a spatula until just combined (lumps are fine). Do not overmix.
4. Heat a non-stick griddle or pan over medium heat and grease lightly with butter.
5. Pour 1/4 cup of batter per pancake onto the griddle.
6. Cook until bubbles form on the surface and the edges look dry (approx. 2-3 minutes). Flip and cook the other side for another 1-2 minutes until golden.
7. Serve warm with butter and maple syrup.
