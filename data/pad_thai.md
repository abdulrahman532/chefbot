# Authentic Pad Thai
## Category: Asian
## Prep Time: 20 mins
## Cook Time: 10 mins
## Difficulty: Hard

## Description
Classic Thai stir-fried rice noodles with tofu, shrimp, bean sprouts, peanuts, and scrambled egg in a tangy tamarind sauce.

## Ingredients
- 200g flat rice noodles
- 150g firm tofu, cubed
- 100g shrimp, peeled and deveined
- 2 tbsp tamarind paste
- 2 tbsp fish sauce
- 1 tbsp brown sugar
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 egg, beaten
- 1 cup bean sprouts
- 3 green onions, chopped
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- 1/4 cup crushed roasted peanuts
- Lime wedges for serving

## Instructions
1. Soak rice noodles in warm water for 30 minutes until soft, then drain.
2. In a small bowl, whisk together tamarind paste, fish sauce, and brown sugar to make the sauce.
3. Heat oil in a large wok over high heat. Cook tofu cubes and shrimp until shrimp turns pink and tofu is browned. Set aside.
4. Add garlic to the wok and cook for 30 seconds. Push garlic to the side, pour in the beaten egg, and scramble it.
5. Add the soaked noodles, cooked shrimp, tofu, and the tamarind sauce to the wok. Toss everything together on high heat.
6. Stir in bean sprouts and green onions. Toss for 1 more minute.
7. Serve immediately topped with crushed peanuts and lime wedges.
