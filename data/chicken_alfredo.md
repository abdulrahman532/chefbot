# Creamy Chicken Alfredo
## Category: Italian
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 20 mins
## Difficulty: Medium

## Description
Fettuccine pasta tossed in a rich, creamy parmesan sauce, topped with grilled chicken breast slices.

## Ingredients
- 300g fettuccine pasta
- 2 boneless, skinless chicken breasts
- 2 tbsp butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup heavy cream
- 1 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and garlic powder to taste
- Fresh chopped parsley for garnish

## Instructions
1. Season chicken breasts with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Heat olive oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook chicken for 6-7 minutes per side until internal temperature reaches 75°C (165°F). Slice chicken and set aside.
2. Cook fettuccine in boiling salted water according to package instructions. Drain, reserving 1/2 cup pasta water.
3. In the same skillet, melt butter over medium heat. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
4. Pour in heavy cream and bring to a simmer. Cook for 3 minutes until slightly thickened.
5. Whisk in grated parmesan cheese until melted and smooth. Season to taste.
6. Add the cooked pasta and sliced chicken to the sauce, tossing to coat. (If too thick, add a splash of reserved pasta water). Garnish with parsley and serve immediately.
