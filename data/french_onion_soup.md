# Classic French Onion Soup
## Category: Soup
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 1 hour
## Difficulty: Hard

## Description
Rich beef broth packed with deeply caramelized onions, topped with toasted baguette and melted Gruyere cheese.

## Ingredients
- 4 large yellow onions, thinly sliced
- 3 tbsp unsalted butter
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 4 cups high-quality beef broth
- 2 sprigs fresh thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- 1 French baguette, sliced
- 1.5 cups shredded Gruyere cheese
- Salt and black pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Melt butter in a large heavy pot over medium-low heat. Add onions and cook, stirring occasionally, for 45 minutes to 1 hour until rich brown and caramelized.
2. Add garlic and cook for 1 minute.
3. Pour in white wine to deglaze the pot, scraping up any browned bits from the bottom. Simmer until wine evaporated.
4. Add beef broth, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a simmer, cover, and cook for 20 minutes.
5. Preheat broiler. Remove thyme and bay leaf from soup. Season to taste.
6. Ladle soup into oven-safe bowls. Place a slice of toasted baguette on top of each, then cover generously with Gruyere cheese.
7. Place under the broiler for 3-4 minutes until the cheese is melted, bubbly, and golden brown.
