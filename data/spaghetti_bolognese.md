# Classic Spaghetti Bolognese
## Category: Italian
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 45 mins
## Difficulty: Medium

## Description
A delicious, rich, slow-simmered Italian beef ragu served over spaghetti.

## Ingredients
- 400g spaghetti
- 500g ground beef
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1 carrot, finely chopped
- 1 celery stalk, finely chopped
- 400g canned crushed tomatoes
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 100ml red wine (optional)
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- Salt and black pepper to taste
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Grated parmesan cheese for serving

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pan over medium heat. Add onion, garlic, carrot, and celery. Cook until soft (approx. 5 minutes).
2. Add the ground beef and cook until browned, breaking it up with a spoon.
3. Pour in the red wine (if using) and simmer until reduced by half.
4. Stir in the tomato paste, crushed tomatoes, dried oregano, salt, and pepper.
5. Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for 35-40 minutes, stirring occasionally.
6. Meanwhile, cook the spaghetti in boiling salted water according to package instructions.
7. Drain spaghetti and serve with the Bolognese sauce spooned over the top. Garnish with grated parmesan.
