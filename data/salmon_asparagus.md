# Garlic Butter Salmon with Asparagus
## Category: Seafood
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 15 mins
## Difficulty: Medium

## Description
Juicy salmon fillets pan-seared with fresh asparagus in a rich garlic butter sauce.

## Ingredients
- 2 salmon fillets (approx. 180g each)
- 1 bunch asparagus, woody ends trimmed
- 2 tbsp butter
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tbsp lemon juice
- Lemon slices for garnish
- Salt, pepper, and paprika to taste

## Instructions
1. Season salmon fillets with salt, pepper, and paprika.
2. Heat olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
3. Add salmon skin-side down and cook for 4-5 minutes until crispy. Flip and cook for another 3-4 minutes until cooked through. Transfer salmon to a plate.
4. Add remaining 1 tbsp butter to the same skillet. Add asparagus and cook for 4-5 minutes until tender-crisp.
5. Add the minced garlic and cook for 1 minute until fragrant.
6. Pour in the lemon juice and stir to combine. Return the salmon to the skillet, spoon the garlic butter over the salmon and asparagus, and garnish with lemon slices before serving.
