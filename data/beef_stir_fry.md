# Quick Beef Stir Fry
## Category: Asian
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 10 mins
## Difficulty: Medium

## Description
Tender strips of beef stir-fried with fresh vegetables in a savory soy-sesame sauce.

## Ingredients
- 400g beef sirloin, thinly sliced across the grain
- 2 cups broccoli florets
- 1 bell pepper, sliced
- 1 carrot, sliced into julienne strips
- 2 tbsp soy sauce
- 1 tbsp sesame oil
- 1 tbsp honey
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp grated ginger
- 1 tbsp cornstarch
- 2 tbsp vegetable oil
- Sesame seeds and sliced green onions for garnish

## Instructions
1. In a small bowl, whisk together soy sauce, sesame oil, honey, garlic, ginger, and cornstarch. Set aside.
2. Heat vegetable oil in a large skillet or wok over high heat.
3. Add the beef slices and cook quickly until browned (approx. 2-3 minutes). Remove beef and set aside.
4. Add broccoli, bell pepper, and carrot to the same skillet. Cook for 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
5. Return the beef to the skillet and pour in the sauce.
6. Stir-fry for 1-2 minutes until the sauce bubbles and thickens. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onions.
