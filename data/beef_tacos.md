# Easy Ground Beef Tacos
## Category: Mexican
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 15 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Quick and flavorful ground beef served in hard or soft taco shells with classic toppings.

## Ingredients
- 500g ground beef
- 1 packet (30g) taco seasoning
- 1/2 cup water
- 8 taco shells (hard or soft)
- 1 cup shredded lettuce
- 1 cup diced tomatoes
- 1 cup shredded cheddar cheese
- 1/2 cup sour cream

## Instructions
1. In a large skillet over medium-high heat, cook ground beef for 8-10 minutes until fully browned. Drain excess fat.
2. Stir in the taco seasoning and water. Bring to a simmer, then reduce heat and cook for 5 minutes until liquid is mostly absorbed.
3. Warm taco shells in the oven or microwave according to package instructions.
4. Assemble tacos by spooning beef into shells, then top with lettuce, tomatoes, cheese, and sour cream as desired.
