# Classic Margherita Pizza
## Category: Italian
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 10 mins
## Difficulty: Medium

## Description
Simple and classic pizza topped with tomato sauce, fresh mozzarella, and basil leaves.

## Ingredients
- 1 pre-made pizza dough ball (approx. 250g)
- 1/2 cup canned crushed San Marzano tomatoes
- 150g fresh mozzarella cheese, sliced or torn
- Handful of fresh basil leaves
- 1 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- Pinch of salt

## Instructions
1. Preheat your oven to the highest setting (usually 250°C/480°F) with a pizza stone or baking sheet inside.
2. Roll or stretch the pizza dough on a floured surface to form a 12-inch circle.
3. Spread the crushed tomatoes evenly over the dough, leaving a 1-inch border.
4. Arrange mozzarella slices on top of the sauce.
5. Carefully transfer the pizza onto the preheated baking sheet or stone.
6. Bake for 8-10 minutes until the crust is golden brown and the cheese is bubbly and slightly browned.
7. Remove from oven, top immediately with fresh basil leaves, drizzle with olive oil, sprinkle with a pinch of salt, slice and serve.
