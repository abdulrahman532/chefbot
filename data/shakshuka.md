# Easy Tunisian Shakshuka
## Category: Breakfast
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 20 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Poached eggs in a rich, spiced tomato and bell pepper sauce.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 medium onion, chopped
- 1 red bell pepper, chopped
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp paprika
- 1 can (400g) crushed tomatoes
- 4 large eggs
- Salt and pepper to taste
- Fresh cilantro or parsley for garnish
- 50g feta cheese, crumbled (optional)

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large skillet over medium heat. Saute onion and bell pepper until soft (approx. 5 minutes).
2. Add garlic, cumin, and paprika, stirring for 1 minute.
3. Pour in the crushed tomatoes and season with salt and pepper. Simmer for 10 minutes until the sauce thickens.
4. Use a spoon to make four small wells in the sauce. Crack an egg into each well.
5. Cover the skillet and cook over low heat for 5-8 minutes until egg whites are set but yolks are still runny.
6. Remove from heat, garnish with fresh herbs and crumbled feta if using, and serve warm with crusty bread.
