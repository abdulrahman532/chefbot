# Traditional Greek Salad
## Category: Salad
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 0 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
A refreshing salad made of tomatoes, cucumbers, red onion, kalamata olives, and a slab of creamy feta cheese.

## Ingredients
- 3 large ripe tomatoes, cut into wedges
- 1 English cucumber, sliced into half-moons
- 1/2 red onion, thinly sliced
- 1/2 cup Kalamata olives
- 200g feta cheese block
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 3 tbsp extra virgin olive oil
- 1 tbsp red wine vinegar
- Salt to taste

## Instructions
1. In a large serving bowl, combine the tomatoes, cucumber, red onion, and olives.
2. Drizzle with the olive oil and red wine vinegar, and sprinkle with a pinch of salt. Toss gently to mix.
3. Place the feta cheese block on top of the salad.
4. Sprinkle the feta and vegetables with dried oregano and another light drizzle of olive oil. Serve cold.
