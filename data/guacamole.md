# Fresh Guacamole
## Category: Appetizer
## Prep Time: 10 mins
## Cook Time: 0 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Authentic, creamy, and chunky Mexican avocado dip with fresh lime, cilantro, and onion.

## Ingredients
- 3 ripe Hass avocados, halved and pitted
- 1 juice of a lime
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1/2 cup diced onion
- 3 tbsp chopped fresh cilantro
- 1 Roma tomato, seeded and diced
- 1 clove garlic, minced (optional)
- Tortilla chips for serving

## Instructions
1. Scoop avocado flesh into a medium bowl.
2. Add the lime juice and salt, then mash with a fork or potato masher to your desired chunkiness.
3. Stir in the diced onion, fresh cilantro, diced tomato, and garlic if using.
4. Taste and adjust salt or lime juice if needed.
5. Serve immediately with tortilla chips or cover tightly with plastic wrap directly touching the surface to prevent browning.
