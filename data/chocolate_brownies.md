# Fudge Chocolate Brownies
## Category: Dessert
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 22 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
Rich, chewy, and fudgy brownies with a shiny crackly top.

## Ingredients
- 200g unsalted butter, melted
- 2 cups granulated sugar
- 4 large eggs
- 1 tsp vanilla extract
- 3/4 cup unsweetened cocoa powder
- 1 cup all-purpose flour
- 1/2 tsp salt
- 1 cup chocolate chips (optional)

## Instructions
1. Preheat oven to 175°C (350°F) and grease a 9x13 inch baking pan.
2. In a large bowl, whisk melted butter, sugar, eggs, and vanilla extract until well combined.
3. Stir in the cocoa powder, flour, and salt until just combined (do not overmix).
4. Fold in the chocolate chips if using.
5. Spread the batter evenly into the prepared pan.
6. Bake for 20-22 minutes. The center should still be slightly soft for a fudgy texture.
7. Allow to cool completely before cutting into squares.
