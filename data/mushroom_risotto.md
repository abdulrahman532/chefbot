# Creamy Mushroom Risotto
## Category: Italian
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 30 mins
## Difficulty: Hard

## Description
Rich and creamy northern Italian rice dish slow-cooked with mixed mushrooms and parmesan.

## Ingredients
- 1.5 cups Arborio rice
- 250g mixed mushrooms (cremini, shiitake, or button), sliced
- 1 medium onion, finely chopped
- 2 garlic cloves, minced
- 1/2 cup dry white wine
- 4 cups warm vegetable broth
- 3 tbsp butter
- 1/2 cup grated parmesan cheese
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- Fresh parsley, chopped

## Instructions
1. Heat 1 tbsp olive oil and 1 tbsp butter in a large pan over medium-high heat. Cook the mushrooms until browned and soft (approx. 5 minutes). Set aside.
2. Add the remaining oil and onion to the pan. Cook until translucent.
3. Add garlic and Arborio rice, stirring constantly for 2 minutes to toast the rice grains.
4. Pour in the white wine and cook until absorbed.
5. Add a ladleful of warm vegetable broth to the rice, stirring continuously until the liquid is almost fully absorbed.
6. Continue adding broth one ladle at a time, stirring frequently. This process should take 20 minutes until the rice is creamy and al dente.
7. Stir in the cooked mushrooms, remaining butter, and parmesan. Garnish with parsley and serve warm.
