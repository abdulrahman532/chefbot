# Classic Minestrone Soup
## Category: Soup
## Prep Time: 15 mins
## Cook Time: 30 mins
## Difficulty: Easy

## Description
A thick, traditional Italian vegetable soup with beans, pasta, and herbs.

## Ingredients
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- 1 onion, diced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 celery stalks, diced
- 2 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 zucchini, diced
- 1 can (400g) diced tomatoes
- 1 can (400g) cannellini beans, rinsed and drained
- 4 cups vegetable broth
- 1 tsp dried oregano
- 1/2 cup small pasta shells
- 1 cup fresh spinach leaves
- Salt and pepper to taste

## Instructions
1. Heat olive oil in a large pot over medium heat. Cook onion, carrots, and celery for 5 minutes.
2. Add garlic and zucchini, cooking for another 2 minutes.
3. Add diced tomatoes, beans, vegetable broth, and oregano. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat and simmer covered for 15 minutes.
4. Add pasta and cook uncovered for 10 minutes until pasta is tender.
5. Stir in spinach and cook for 2 minutes until wilted. Season with salt and pepper, and serve hot.
